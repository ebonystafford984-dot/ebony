Problem Statement

IT data-management professionals often spend time reconciling duplicate or inconsistent records across systems that use different data standards. These discrepancies can delay reporting workflows and lead to inaccurate organizational reports, affecting the teams that rely on timely, trustworthy data.

Research Question

What does current research tell us about the effects of inconsistent or duplicate records on reporting workflows for IT data-management professionals in organizations that use multiple information systems?

Sources to Explore

I plan to search Google Scholar and the ACM Digital Library for research on data quality, duplicate records, and reporting workflows.
